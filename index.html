about

I'm an embedded systems engineer who likes getting close to the hardware — writing firmware that talks directly to ADCs, EEPROMs, and communication buses, and reasoning about timing, power, and reliability at the register level. Most of what I build lives somewhere between an FPGA fabric and a microcontroller's peripheral map.

projects

Battery Telemetry Logger

View repository

A self-contained battery telemetry system built on an STM32F0Discovery board running FreeRTOS. HAL-based drivers handle I2C and SPI EEPROMs for persistent logging and calibration storage, while ADC-driven sensor simulation feeds a software coulomb counter. Two firmware roles communicate over a UART command/response link.
